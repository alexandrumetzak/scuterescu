Who we are

What data we collect

This website has no forms, uses no cookies and no analytics tools. The website itself does not collect personal data, except for technical data (for example the IP address), processed by the hosting provider to deliver the pages.

When you call us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number and the information you send (for example your rental dates). When you rent a scooter, we process the data on your ID document and driving licence, as required for the rental contract.

Why we use the data and on what legal basis

We use the data to answer your requests and to conclude and perform the rental contract, on the legal basis of performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — GDPR). We also use the data to meet legal obligations, for example tax and accounting obligations, on the legal basis of a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Who we share data with

When you message us on WhatsApp, your message is transmitted through the Meta Platforms Ireland Limited platform; Meta may transfer data outside the European Economic Area under its own data-protection safeguards. Our hosting provider (GitHub, Inc.) processes technical data (for example the IP address) in server logs to deliver the pages. Contract data may be shared with our accountant or with the authorities, where required by law.

How long we keep the data

We keep contract data for the period required by tax and accounting law. Phone and WhatsApp messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. To exercise them, contact us at +40 756 205 206. You may also lodge a complaint with the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro.
