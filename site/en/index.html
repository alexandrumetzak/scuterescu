Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a scooter in Iași?

A 50cc scooter costs 70 RON per day or 300 RON per week. A 125cc scooter costs 80 RON per day or 350 RON per week. Third-party (RCA) insurance is included.

What licence do I need for a 50cc scooter?

For a 50cc scooter you need a category AM or B driving licence. A category B licence includes category AM.

What licence do I need for a 125cc scooter?

For a 125cc scooter you need a category A1 or A driving licence.

How much is the deposit?

The deposit is 300 RON for 50cc scooters and 350 RON for 125cc scooters. You pay it at pick-up and get it back when you return the scooter.

What is included in the price?

The price includes third-party (RCA) insurance. A helmet is free on weekly rentals and costs 10 RON per rental on the daily rate.

Do I get a helmet?

Yes. A helmet is free on weekly rentals. On daily rentals, a helmet costs 10 RON per rental.

What is the minimum age to rent a scooter?

The minimum age to rent a scooter is 18.

What documents do I need?

You need an ID card or passport and a driving licence valid for the scooter category.

Where do I pick up and return the scooter?

You pick up and return the scooter at Str. Al. O. Teodoreanu nr. 49, Iași. Opening hours are Monday–Friday 09:00–19:00 and Saturday–Sunday 12:00–19:00.

How do I book a scooter?

Call +40 756 205 206 or send a WhatsApp message to the same number with your dates and the scooter type: 50cc or 125cc.
