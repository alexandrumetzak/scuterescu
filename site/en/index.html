Frequently asked questions.

How much does it cost to rent a scooter in Iași?

The price depends on the rental length: 1–2 days cost 90 RON/day (50cc) or 100 RON/day (125cc), 3–6 days cost 79 RON/day or 89 RON/day, and from 7 days it is 45 RON/day or 50 RON/day. Helmet and RCA insurance are included.

What licence do I need for a 50cc scooter?

For a 50cc scooter you need a category AM or B licence.

What licence do I need for a 125cc scooter?

For a 125cc scooter you need a category A1 or A licence, or a category B licence if you are at least 24 and have completed the required course.

How much is the deposit?

The deposit is 300 RON for 50cc scooters and 350 RON for 125cc scooters. It is paid at pick-up and refunded when you return the scooter.

What is included in the price?

Every rate includes the helmet, RCA insurance, the anti-theft system, 24/7 roadside assistance and unlimited kilometres. Rentals of at least 7 days also include a phone holder.

What extras can I add?

You can add an extra passenger helmet for 20 RON and a phone holder for 10 RON, for the whole rental period. The phone holder is free from 7 days.

What is the minimum age to rent a scooter?

The minimum age to rent a scooter is 18. For a 125cc scooter ridden on a category B licence, the minimum age is 24.

What documents do I need to bring?

You need your ID card or passport and a driving licence valid for the scooter's category.

Where do I pick up and return the scooter?

You pick up and return the scooter at Str. Al. O. Teodoreanu nr. 49, Iași. Hours are Monday–Friday 09:00–19:00, Saturday and Sunday 12:00–19:00.

How do I book a scooter?

Fill in the booking form on the site, which opens WhatsApp with the details, or call +40 756 205 206. The booking is confirmed on WhatsApp, subject to availability.
